Compatibility & proof

Standalone PRG includes model, artwork, original SID score and INFO. No secondary in-game loads, network or host inference. D64 contains the same PRG.

0-player mode continuously replays after a short result hold. Every move runs the frozen model. Later openings randomize only its approved-set tie; this is a WarGames-inspired demonstration, not learning or temperature scaling. Q exits at safe turn boundaries.

Emulator-tested only; physical C64/1541 unverified. Decorative lights and sound are not model explanations. Loader, browser interaction and numerical gates have separate evidence scopes.
